01Executive Summary
1.1 Purpose
Financial institutions process millions of financial transactions every day. Existing fraud
detection systems are highly effective at detecting anomalies but fail to answer the
most important question:
Why is this transaction suspicious?
Most existing systems produce only a numerical risk score or a binary fraud flag.
These outputs provide almost no contextual information to investigators.
Consequently,
investigators manually inspect customer history,
transaction history,
merchant behavior,
device information,
geolocation,
historical fraud cases,
before making a decision.
This  process  can  take  between  30  minutes  and  several  hours for  a  single
investigation.
1.2 Product Vision
FFIRE transforms fraud detection into an explainable investigation platform.
Instead of producing only a fraud score, FFIRE performs the investigation itself using
deterministic AI reasoning.
Every conclusion produced by the AI must be backed by retrieved evidence.
No unsupported statements are allowed.
Every decision is auditable.
Every investigation is reproducible.

03Business Goals
3.1 Business Objective
FFIRE is designed to transform fraud investigation from a manual, analyst-driven process
into a deterministic, AI-assisted workflow that increases investigator productivity without
compromising explainability or regulatory compliance.
The product does not replace fraud analysts. Instead, it automates evidence collection,
correlation, reasoning, and report generation, allowing analysts to focus on judgment
and decision-making rather than repetitive investigation tasks.
3.2 Business Goals
BG-01 Reduce Investigation Time
Current State: - Average investigation duration: 30–120 minutes - Manual retrieval from
multiple systems - Manual evidence correlation
T arget: - Standard investigation completed in under 8 seconds - Complex investigation
completed in under 30 seconds - >80% reduction in analyst effort
BG-02 Improve Investigation Quality
Every investigation must:
retrieve all available evidence
explain every conclusion
provide confidence scores
cite supporting evidence
produce consistent results
Success Criteria:
100% grounded conclusions
No unsupported AI-generated claims
BG-03 Reduce Operational Cost
Reduce the number of manual investigation hours by automating repetitive tasks.
