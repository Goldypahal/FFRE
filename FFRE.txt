Product: Financial Fraud Investigation Reasoning Engine (FFIRE)
Chapter 1 — Executive Summary
1.1 Purpose
Financial institutions process millions of financial transactions every day. Existing fraud detection systems are highly effective at detecting anomalies but fail to answer the most important question:
Why is this transaction suspicious?
Most existing systems produce only a numerical risk score or a binary fraud flag.
These outputs provide almost no contextual information to investigators.
Consequently,
investigators manually inspect customer history, 
transaction history, 
merchant behavior, 
device information, 
geolocation, 
historical fraud cases, 
before making a decision.
This process can take between 30 minutes and several hours for a single investigation.
1.2 Product Vision
FFIRE transforms fraud detection into an explainable investigation platform.
Instead of producing only a fraud score, FFIRE performs the investigation itself using deterministic AI reasoning.
Every conclusion produced by the AI must be backed by retrieved evidence.
No unsupported statements are allowed.
Every decision is auditable.
Every investigation is reproducible.
1.3 Primary Goals
The product aims to reduce investigation effort while increasing investigator confidence.
Goals include
reduce investigation time by over 80% 
reduce false positive reviews 
provide complete explainability 
create audit-ready reports 
maintain deterministic AI execution 
enable regulatory compliance 
1.4 Product Philosophy
Unlike conversational AI assistants, FFIRE never "guesses."
Instead it follows a predefined reasoning graph.

↓

↓

↓
Analysis
↓

↓

↓

↓

Every stage is observable.
Every state transition is logged.
Every output can be reproduced.

1.6 Product Principles
The system follows six guiding principles.
Explainability
Every statement must reference evidence.
Determinism
Identical inputs produce identical investigations.
Transparency
Users can inspect every reasoning step.
Auditability
Every decision is logged permanently.
Security
Sensitive information remains encrypted.
Role-based access control is enforced.
Human Oversight
AI never makes irreversible decisions.
Low-confidence investigations require human review.
1.7 Success Definition
A successful investigation satisfies all of the following:
✔ Investigation completed
✔ Evidence retrieved
✔ All claims validated
✔ Confidence calculated
✔ Report generated
✔ Audit log stored
✔ Analyst notified

14.2 Design Principles
The design system follows five principles.
Principle 1 — Information Density
Fraud analysts inspect hundreds of investigations every day.
Large empty spaces reduce efficiency.
The dashboard should maximize useful information while maintaining readability.

Chapter 44 — Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Bounded Contexts & Microservice Decomposition
44.1 Purpose
This chapter defines the Domain-Driven Design (DDD) architecture of FFIRE.

Every business capability has a single owner. 
Each microservice owns its own data. 
Domain logic is isolated. 
Teams can develop independently. 
Services evolve without creating a distributed monolith. 
FFIRE follows Strategic DDD for service boundaries and Tactical DDD within each bounded context.
